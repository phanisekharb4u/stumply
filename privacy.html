Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Who we are

Stumply (stumply.pages.dev) is a free website for running cricket tournaments and sharing live scores. It is run by Phani Sekhar S. If you have any question about your data, write to phanisekharb4u@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account details for tournament admins: your name, the username you choose, and your email address. If you sign in with Google, X (Twitter) or Facebook, we receive your name, email address and an account ID from that service. We never see or store your password for those services.
  • Tournament data that admins enter: tournament names, teams, player names, optional photos, match schedules, scores and results.
  • Basic activity: when an admin account was created and last used, so the site owner can see how many people use Stumply.

Viewers who only watch scores do not need an account, and we do not collect anything about them.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your tournaments under your account.
  • To show scores, scorecards and stats to anyone who has your tournament's share link or username. Tournament data is public to anyone with the link, so do not add anything you want to keep private.
  • If you switch on Show on the Discover page, the tournament name, edition, city, number of teams and your username are listed publicly so people can find it. It is off unless you turn it on.
  • If you add a co-scorer, their username is saved with the tournament so they can score it.
  • To send password reset emails when you ask for one.

We do not sell your data, show ads, or share it with anyone for marketing.

Where it is stored

Data is stored with Google Firebase (Authentication and Realtime Database, Singapore region). The website is served by Cloudflare. Your browser also keeps your sign-in and your language choice on your own device so you stay signed in.

Deleting your data

You can delete matches, teams and whole tournaments yourself from the Admin area at any time.

To delete your whole account and everything linked to it, including data received from Google, X or Facebook, email us from the address on your account with the subject "Delete my Stumply account". We will delete it within 30 days and reply to confirm.

If you signed in with Facebook, you can also remove Stumply from your Facebook settings under Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites, and then email us as above so we delete what we hold.

Request account deletion

Children

Admin accounts are meant for people aged 13 and over. Tournament organisers should get permission before adding the names or photos of young players.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
